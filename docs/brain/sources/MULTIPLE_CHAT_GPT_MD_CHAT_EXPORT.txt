## Client communication

### Useful principle

When a client is busy, repeatedly asking “Have you signed?” can create pressure without moving the project forward.

A better approach is to remove friction:

* Offer to review the contract together.
* Break information collection into manageable pieces.
* Ask them to send whatever is already available.
* Organise incomplete information yourself where possible.
* Avoid repeatedly restarting the same information request.

### Tone

The preferred client tone is:

* Respectful.
* Direct.
* Human.
* Calm.
* Not submissive.
* Not desperate.
* Not excessively formal.
